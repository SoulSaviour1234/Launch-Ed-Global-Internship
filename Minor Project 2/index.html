About Tandoor Grid

Founded in 2010, Tandoor Grid is a culinary haven that celebrates the diverse and vibrant flavors of India. We believe in using traditional cooking methods, like our authentic clay tandoor oven, to craft dishes that are true to their roots.

Every spice is hand-ground, and every recipe carries a story passed down through generations. Join us to experience the genuine warmth of Indian hospitality.
