Our Story

Started in 2026, CraveWave had a very simple idea: everyone deserves to eat great foreign food at home. Our founders, who love to eat and travel, thought of bringing local chefs and hungry people together.

Our Mission and Values

  • Pure Taste: We only work with chefs who make food the traditional way.
  • Easy Reach: Bringing world-class flavors to every street and corner.
  • Nature Friendly: Using green packing for every single delivery.
  • Helping Locals: Supporting our local chefs and small food stalls.
